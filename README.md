In my last role on an R&D team I shipped a live freelance marketplace (profiles, in-chat proposals, milestone escrow on Stripe), built an internal n8n-style AI agent platform (LINE webhooks, OpenAI agent nodes, BullMQ), and redesigned a legacy Java/JSP manufacturing ERP with shadcn/ui, cutting the AP workflow from 6 steps to 3.

Right now I'm focused on **AI-powered apps that remove manual work**, like turning a photo of a receipt into a structured record.

#### 🧾 AI Expense Tracker &nbsp;[![Backend](https://img.shields.io/badge/backend-repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/wunnaaung-dev/ai-expense-tracker) [![Frontend](https://img.shields.io/badge/frontend-repo-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/wunnaaung-dev/ai-expense-tracker-frontend)
